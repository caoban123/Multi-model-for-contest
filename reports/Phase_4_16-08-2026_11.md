## Default Decision

Decision: **HYBRID OPT-IN**.

Rationale:

- Structured implementation/evidence/debug paths are useful and verified technically.
- Object and metadata relevance improvements have not been manually established.
- General-semantic non-regression cannot be measured without judgements.
- Object tail latency and 20.359% missing coverage require caution.
- Historical object-fusion experiments reported regression, so automatic promotion would contradict available evidence.

Enforcement:

- `/api/search` remains the Phase-3 CLIP default.
- `/api/structured-search` is a separate experimental route.
- Structured Filters are collapsed and unchecked by default.
- No RRF weights or confidence threshold are declared final.
