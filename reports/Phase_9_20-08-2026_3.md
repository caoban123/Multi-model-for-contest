## Risks

- Object-heavy corpus composition may bias dense/lexical retrieval toward generic labels such as person/building.
- Long metadata can be truncated silently if model token limits are not captured in the manifest.
- ASR noise may produce semantically misleading dense matches.
- OCR absence means no hybrid method can recover text visible only on screen from current artifacts.
