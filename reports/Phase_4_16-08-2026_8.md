## Data Status

Evidence contract per result:

- CLIP: enabled, status, rank and original similarity score.
- Objects: enabled, status, video coverage, rank and matched detection evidence.
- Metadata: enabled, status, video-level marker, rank and matched fields.
- Fusion: method and complete centralized RRF config.
- Representative: exact rule used.

Allowed modality statuses are `matched`, `not_matched`, `unknown`, `disabled`. Fixture coverage exercises all four values.
