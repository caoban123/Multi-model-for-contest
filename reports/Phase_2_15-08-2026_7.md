## Current Status

The L21 retrieval UI now supports a complete lightweight review loop:

- Search visual CLIP results.
- Optionally translate Vietnamese query to an English CLIP query.
- Judge results as `Good`, `Partial`, or `Bad`.
- Add review notes.
- Export review CSV.
- Pin important candidates.
- Re-run recent searches from browser-local history.
- Export pinned candidates separately.
