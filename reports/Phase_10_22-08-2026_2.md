## Runtime Changes

### Q&A

- Candidate videos are deterministically enriched with representative frame fields, image/video URLs, metadata and retrieval provenance before evidence-pack construction.
- Metadata is loaded for every candidate instead of depending on an earlier cache access.
- Evidence limits now apply per video and modality, so lower-ranked candidate videos do not silently lose all keyframe evidence after the first eight global items.
- Draft and review reject unknown evidence IDs and reject evidence spanning more than one video.
- Submission import repeats the same-video check, including for legacy persisted reviews.
- `/submission` renders Q&A candidate cards with rank, score, title, author, date, frame diagnostics, zoom, raw video, copy and neighborhood controls.
- One active video controls the visible evidence set. Switching video deliberately resets the draft and official frame input.

### TRAKE

- `/submission` exposes event text editing, required/optional selection, add, remove and reorder controls.
- Saving a plan calls `/api/trake/update-plan`, then reruns search and alignment.
- Each selected chain event exposes up to ten same-video candidates with frame preview, score and Replace + lock.
- Replacement remains server-validated against same-video and temporal invariants.
- Per-event bounded refine calls `/api/trake/refine` and displays its status/image without silently replacing the official answer.
- Manual chains and locked event state remain visible after replacement.
