python tools/phase7_benchmark.py --queries benchmarks/phase7_qa_queries_v1.json --split holdout --qa-store artifacts/qa/phase7_qa.sqlite3 --output artifacts/benchmarks/phase7/holdout.json

- `availability: unavailable`
