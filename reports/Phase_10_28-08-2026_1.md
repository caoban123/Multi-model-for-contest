## Decisions

- Keep CLIP as the default visual retriever.
- Keep BGE, BM25, OCR/ASR and RRF modular and intent-gated.
- Use the Phase 5 store and BM25 v2 for current OCR retrieval.
- Preserve the old BGE index until a GPU rebuild and benchmark are complete.
- Treat missing/unmapped OCR and uncovered ASR as `UNAVAILABLE`, never `NO_MATCH`.
- Do not fabricate ASR from the OCR-only package.
- Keep human review and official frame selection in the submission workflow.

## Risks

- Nearest BTC keyframe may be up to 5 seconds from the OCR source frame; provenance exposes the distance for review.
- Logo/text repetition can lower lexical diversity without a dedup stage.
- Full OCR increases store/corpus memory and disk use; large artifacts remain on SSD E rather than Git.
- Gemini remains Internet/API dependent and is not required for local OCR search.
