## Risks

- Incomplete OCR/ASR coverage can make BGE/BM25 appear weak for reasons unrelated to retriever quality.
- Joining text hits to the wrong representative frame can improve video recall while producing invalid submission frames.
- Tuning weights on a small hand-picked query set can overfit and regress unseen contest queries.
- Loading CLIP and a large BGE model together can exceed available RAM/VRAM or increase UI latency.
- Gemini/API-dependent planning must retain a deterministic fallback for contest-time connectivity failures.
