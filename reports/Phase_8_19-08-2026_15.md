# Phase 8 TRAKE V2 — P8.V2.8 Multimodal + P8.V2.9 Context + P8.V2.10 Linkage

## Checkpoint scope

These three adjacent scoring checkpoints share the same late evidence boundary and are therefore reported together. None of them performs a new corpus-wide extraction or an early hard filter.

## P8.V2.8 — event-aware multimodal score

- Added `event-aware-reciprocal-rank-v1` normalization.
- Raw modality scores and ranks remain separate and inspectable.
- Only modalities routed by the event planner participate.
- OCR/ASR receive dominant starting weights, object/attribute supporting weights, and visual CLIP remains the visual base; every weight is explicit in `configs/phase8_trake_v2.json`.
- AVAILABLE channels with no match contribute zero; UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE channels are omitted rather than silently treated as negative evidence.
- The DANTE semantic term now uses this event-aware normalized score.
- SigLIP remains disabled/unavailable; no model was downloaded.

## P8.V2.9 — global context

- Added independent late `context_score` with method/version, per-modality status, matched tokens, and components.
- It reads only verified candidate evidence already present for metadata, ASR, OCR, or object channels.
- Missing evidence yields `UNKNOWN`/`null`, not zero.
- Context is applied during final reranking, after temporal chains exist, so it cannot destroy retrieval recall early.
- No corpus-wide caption generation was introduced.

## P8.V2.10 — adjacent-frame linkage

- Added explicit adjacent-pair components for temporal proximity, visual continuity, scene continuity, object/entity continuity, and ASR continuity.
- Temporal proximity uses a smooth exponential score with a configurable scale; user hard gaps remain enforced in alignment.
- Visual/scene/object/ASR components are evaluated only when their evidence is present. Otherwise they remain `UNKNOWN` or `UNAVAILABLE` and are not converted into negative evidence.
- The aggregate and every pair/component are persisted in the chain score breakdown.

## Verification

- New scoring tests cover event routing, cross-scale rank normalization, raw-value preservation, UNKNOWN omission, context match vs unknown, and all linkage availability states.
- Focused scoring/DANTE/temporal/config subset: `23 passed in 1.35s`.
- Full focused TRAKE suite after integration: `73 passed in 2.93s`.

## Evidence boundary

These fixture tests prove transformation, routing, and ordering behavior. They do not establish retrieval-quality improvement. CLIP-only vs multimodal quality remains unmeasured until labelled development queries exist; the later ablation runner reports null quality fields in their absence.
