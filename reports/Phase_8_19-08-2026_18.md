## Annotation support added

- `benchmarks/trake_annotation_template_v2.json` with PTS interval labels and no prefilled video/timestamp truth.
- `docs/PHASE8_ANNOTATION_V2.md` with independent manual annotation, second-review, development-only tuning, and holdout freeze rules.
- `tools/phase8_validate_annotations.py` validates:
  - L21 video IDs;
  - 1–5 unique ordered events;
  - required-event PTS intervals;
  - category vocabulary;
  - annotator/timestamp metadata;
  - monotonic interval order;
  - frozen development config before labelled holdout use.

Supported categories include simple/2–5 event, generic/distinctive order, OCR, ASR, object, color attribute, gap lengths, same/cross scene, ambiguous, and OOD/rare entity.
