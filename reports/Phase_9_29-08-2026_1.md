## Retrieval Findings

The Vietnamese query about a boarding house for elderly people produced two materially different outcomes:

1. Unrestricted BGE retrieval ranked the expected candidate video `L30_V043` first through video-level metadata.
2. Global OCR-only BGE retrieval ranked `L22_V017` first and did not return `L30_V043` in the top 20. The result was semantically attracted to repeated OCR text about elderly centers rather than the target event.

This is evidence that source filtering must be staged. OCR/ASR should refine a shortlist of candidate videos instead of replacing video discovery across the complete collection.
