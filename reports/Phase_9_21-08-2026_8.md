## Seven-profile Ablation Caveat

The full CLIP/BGE/BM25 ablation executes successfully after multi-group and keyframe contract fixes. Its CLIP rows are not valid quality measurements for this query set: source-type filters target ASR/metadata text, and the CLIP adapter correctly returns no hits for those channels. Therefore CLIP MRR 0 in this artifact means `filtered out`, not `CLIP retrieval failed`.

The ablation artifact is useful as an execution/contract smoke only. It must not be used to compare visual quality or promote a default profile.
