- The installed `faiss-cpu 1.8.0` permits a local adapter without adding a dependency.

## Risks

- Metadata/object documents could dominate the text corpus unless provenance and document granularity are controlled.
- The object store contains 780,000 raw detections; indexing every detection as a separate text document would create severe duplication.
- ASR transcript quality is noisy in addition to its low coverage.
- A structurally valid index does not prove retrieval relevance; CLIP quality remains dependent on manual judgements.
